Having said that, we focused on steps that can be taken to improve intracranial perfusion. We discussed about possible role of STA MCA bypass on the right side, with the intention to improve perfusion on the ipsilateral side, so as to supplant the posterior circulation's contribution to the right side. However it is unclear if she will definitively obtain a clinical benefit from this. In case the bypass remains patent and provides optimal supplementation to the perfusion of right anterior circulation, hopefully, it would minimize the risk of recurrent ipsilateral strokes in future. The other important thing of concern would be perioperative risk of ischemia/hypoperfusion from anesthetic medications-induced hemodynamic changes, especially as he is likely to be highly dependent on optimal perfusion pressure and will not tolerate any degree of hypotension.
It is possible she may require a bypass procedure on both sides however this will be assessed in due course of time.
